# index-db v0.6.0 — Alpha

**The soak begins.** v0.6.0 is the alpha in the run to 1.0. The in-memory tree is
feature-complete and API-frozen; this release adds no behaviour and no public
surface. It broadens coverage to a sustained, consumer-shaped workload — the way a
storage engine actually drives a secondary index — and records the benchmark
baselines. From here to 1.0 is soak, polish, and the page-backed backend.

## What is index-db?

A B+tree indexing primitive for storage engines: ordered keys mapped to values,
with fast point lookups, ordered iteration, range scans, and bulk loading, packaged
as a small, audited library. It is the ordered-access and secondary-index layer a
storage engine builds on.

## What's new in 0.6.0

### A sustained, consumer-shaped workload

`tests/soak.rs` drives the tree the way a query-serving index is driven: a
read-heavy mix — roughly 70% point lookups, 12% range scans, 10% inserts, 8%
deletes — over an evolving working set, for 200,000 operations. The operation
stream is generated by a fixed PRNG, so the run is deterministic and reproducible
with no random seed and no `rand` dependency. Every operation's result is checked
against a `BTreeMap` reference as it happens; at regular checkpoints the whole map
is compared in full, forward and reverse; and at the end the tree is drained
key by key back to an empty single leaf. It is a different shape of test from the
v0.5.0 adversarial and stress suites — not pathological orderings or peak sizes,
but the steady, mixed churn of normal use, sustained long enough to surface
anything that only shows up over time.

### Benchmark baselines

`docs/PERFORMANCE.md` records the Criterion baselines for point lookups, inserts,
removes, range scans, and bulk load, with the method and an honest reading of what
the numbers mean — including the cache-bound growth of lookups at scale and the
~1-indirection-per-level cost of the id-addressed node store. Point lookups run
about 12 ns at a thousand entries and 80 ns at a million; range scans cost about a
nanosecond per entry yielded and track the window width rather than the tree size.

### Why no new API

The public surface was frozen at v0.5.0 and stays frozen: `BPlusTree`'s
constructors and operations, the `Default` and `IntoIterator` impls, and the
`Iter` iterator. The alpha phase is for confidence, not features.

## What's next

- **Beta / RC.** Bug fixes only; final benchmarks confirmed; documentation polish.
- **Page-backed backend.** A `PageStore` behind the storage seam, with latch
  coupling (crabbing) over `page-db`'s page guards — the concurrent, persistent
  tree, loom-checked. Blocked on `page-db` publication. Whether 1.0 ships with it
  or adds it additively in 1.x is decided by RC.
- **1.0.0 — Stable.** Definition-of-Done audit and publication.
